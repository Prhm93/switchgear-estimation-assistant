How it works

From three documents to one checked estimate

01

Upload

Specification, drawing and letter are stored unchanged, each with its own fingerprint.

02

Read

Ratings, poles, quantities and part types are found with the exact sentence or drawing text they came from.

03

Cross-check

Where the drawing and specification disagree, both sources are shown side by side. Nothing is chosen quietly.

04

Approve & export

Only approved values reach the estimate. Prices come from the catalogue, and every action is logged.

Measured, not promised

Every step is scored against an answer key

Each method is scored on the same 20 test enquiries. From Stage 4, drawing results are reported separately for clean, scanned, photographed and real drawings.
