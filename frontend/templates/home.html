How it works

From three documents to one checked estimate

01

Upload

Specification, drawing and letter are stored unchanged, each with its own fingerprint.

02

Read

Ratings, poles, quantities and part types are found with the exact sentence or drawing text they came from.

03

Cross-check

Where the drawing and specification disagree, both sources are shown side by side. Nothing is chosen quietly.

04

Approve & export

Only approved values reach the quote. Prices come from the catalogue, and every action is logged.

Measured, not promised

Every method is scored against an answer key

20 invented test enquiries and 20 reworded copies whose wording the reading rules never saw. The scores are F1 over every value. Drawings are scored apart as clean, scanned and photographed; no real drawing has been scored yet.
