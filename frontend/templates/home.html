A customer enquiry arrives as three documents that rarely agree. The assistant reads each one, shows where every value came from, puts disagreements side by side and leaves every decision to the engineer. Nothing reaches the quote until it is approved.

01 · How it works

Three steps, one checked quote

  1. 1

    Files

    Drop in the specification, the drawing and the customer letter. Each is kept unchanged.

  2. 2

    Check

    Every value shows its source. Where documents disagree, both sides are shown and you decide.

  3. 3

    Quote

    Approved values only, catalogue prices only, and a report that says how it was read.

02 · Measured, not promised

Scored against an answer key

20 invented test enquiries and 20 reworded copies, written to test the reading rules on new wording. The scores are F1 over every value. Drawings are scored apart as clean, scanned and photographed; no real drawing has been scored yet.

03 · Trust & safety

The engineer decides

  • Runs locally: no document leaves the machine.
  • Documents are data, never instructions: instruction-like text is flagged, not followed.
  • Every model answer must quote its source; a quote that is not on the page is rejected.
  • Only catalogue prices: prices never come from a model, and every decision is logged.
